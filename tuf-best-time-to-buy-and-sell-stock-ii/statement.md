# Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock II

`arr[i]` is the stock price on day `i`. You may buy and sell any number of times (holding at most one share at a time; you must sell before buying again). Print the maximum total profit.

## Input
- Line 1: `n`.
- Line 2: `n` integers `arr[i]`.

## Output
A single integer: the maximum total profit.

## Constraints
- `1 <= n <= 10^5`
- `0 <= arr[i] <= 10^4`

## Examples
```
Input:
6
9 2 6 4 7 3
Output:
7
```
