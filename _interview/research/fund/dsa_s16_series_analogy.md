# Series, Analogy & Classification

::: keypoint
**The Series Toolkit:** When faced with a sequence, test these patterns in order:

1. **Constant / Arithmetic Difference:** Check the gaps ($T_{n} - T_{n-1}$). If they form an arithmetic progression, keep going.
2. **Geometric / Multiplicative Ratio:** If terms grow quickly, test $T_n = a \times T_{n-1} \pm b$.
3. **Squares / Cubes:** Look for numbers near $n^2$, $n^3$, or $n^2 \pm n$.
4. **Primes:** If differences are irregular but terms are odd, check for prime sequence gaps.
5. **Difference of Differences:** If the first-level differences are not obvious, take the difference of the differences. It often yields a constant.
6. **Alternating / Double Series:** If the series goes up and down (e.g., $5, 10, 4, 12, 3$), split it into two interleaved sequences.
7. **Letter / Number Positions:** Map $A=1, B=2 \ldots Z=26$. Memorize $E=5, J=10, O=15, T=20, Y=25$ (EJOTY) and reverse positions ($27 - n$).
:::

## Number Series

**Q1.** What is the next term in the series: $2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ?$
(a) $36$ (b) $37$ (c) $39$ (d) $41$

**Solution:**
The differences are: $5 - 2 = 3$, $10 - 5 = 5$, $17 - 10 = 7$, $26 - 17 = 9$.
The differences form the arithmetic progression $3, 5, 7, 9$. The next difference is $11$.
The next term is $26 + 11 = 37$.
*Alternatively:* The terms are $n^2 + 1$ for $n=1, 2, 3, 4, 5$. The next term is $6^2 + 1 = 37$.

**Answer:** $37$ (b)

**Q2.** Find the missing term: $3, 7, 15, 31, ?, 127$
(a) $62$ (b) $63$ (c) $64$ (d) $65$

**Solution:**
Each term is generated by multiplying the previous term by $2$ and adding $1$.
$T_n = 2T_{n-1} + 1$.
$3 \times 2 + 1 = 7$.
$7 \times 2 + 1 = 15$.
$15 \times 2 + 1 = 31$.
$31 \times 2 + 1 = 63$.
Verify: $63 \times 2 + 1 = 127$.

**Answer:** $63$ (b)

**Q3.** What will come in place of the question mark: $4, 18, ?, 100, 180, 294, 448$
(a) $38$ (b) $42$ (c) $48$ (d) $58$

**Solution:**
Test standard cubes and squares. The terms follow the pattern $n^3 - n^2$.
For $n=2$: $2^3 - 2^2 = 8 - 4 = 4$.
For $n=3$: $3^3 - 3^2 = 27 - 9 = 18$.
For $n=4$: $4^3 - 4^2 = 64 - 16 = 48$.
For $n=5$: $5^3 - 5^2 = 125 - 25 = 100$.
The missing term is $48$.

**Answer:** $48$ (c)

**Q4.** Find the wrong number in the sequence: $3, 8, 15, 24, 34, 48, 63$
(a) $15$ (b) $24$ (c) $34$ (d) $48$

**Solution:**
The differences are:
$8 - 3 = 5$
$15 - 8 = 7$
$24 - 15 = 9$
The next difference should be $11$. Let's test: $24 + 11 = 35$.
If the term were $35$, the next difference would be $48 - 35 = 13$, which perfectly fits the odd-number sequence $5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15$.
Therefore, $34$ is the wrong number and should be $35$.

**Answer:** $34$ (c)

**Q5.** Find the next term: $2, 3, 5, 7, 11, ?, 17$
(a) $12$ (b) $13$ (c) $14$ (d) $15$

**Solution:**
The sequence consists of consecutive prime numbers.
The prime numbers are $2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17$.
The missing term is $13$.

**Answer:** $13$ (b)

**Q6.** What is the next number: $8, 28, 116, 584, ?$
(a) $1752$ (b) $3504$ (c) $3508$ (d) $3512$

**Solution:**
Test multiplicative growth since the numbers scale rapidly.
$8 \times 3 + 4 = 24 + 4 = 28$.
$28 \times 4 + 4 = 112 + 4 = 116$.
$116 \times 5 + 4 = 580 + 4 = 584$.
The pattern is $T_n = T_{n-1} \times (n+1) + 4$.
The next term is $584 \times 6 + 4 = 3504 + 4 = 3508$.

**Answer:** $3508$ (c)

**Q7.** Find the missing term: $14, 28, 20, 40, 32, 64, ?$
(a) $52$ (b) $56$ (c) $96$ (d) $128$

**Solution:**
This sequence follows a two-step alternating operation: multiply by $2$, then subtract $8$.
$14 \times 2 = 28$.
$28 - 8 = 20$.
$20 \times 2 = 40$.
$40 - 8 = 32$.
$32 \times 2 = 64$.
The next step is to subtract $8$: $64 - 8 = 56$.

::: trap
When a series repeatedly increases and decreases, do not waste time searching for a single arithmetic progression. Immediately check for interleaved sequences or a two-step alternating operation.
:::

**Answer:** $56$ (b)

**Q8.** What is the next term in the series: $1, 2, 6, 15, 31, 56, ?$
(a) $91$ (b) $92$ (c) $93$ (d) $94$

**Solution:**
Find the first-level differences:
$2 - 1 = 1$
$6 - 2 = 4$
$15 - 6 = 9$
$31 - 15 = 16$
$56 - 31 = 25$
The differences are consecutive perfect squares ($1^2, 2^2, 3^2, 4^2, 5^2$).
The next difference is $6^2 = 36$.
The next term is $56 + 36 = 92$.

**Answer:** $92$ (b)

### OA Variant

**Q9.** A distributed system expands its cluster size incrementally. The node count follows the sequence $5, 12, 31, 68, 129, \dots$. If the pattern holds, what is the expected node count in the next increment?
(a) $208$ (b) $215$ (c) $220$ (d) $228$

**Solution:**
Let's analyze the sequence $5, 12, 31, 68, 129$.
Take the first-level differences: $7, 19, 37, 61$.
Take the second-level differences: $12, 18, 24$.
The second-level differences form an arithmetic progression increasing by $6$. The next second-level difference is $24 + 6 = 30$.
The next first-level difference is $61 + 30 = 91$.
The next term is $129 + 91 = 220$.
*Faster trick:* Notice that $5 = 1^3 + 4$, $12 = 2^3 + 4$, $31 = 3^3 + 4$, $68 = 4^3 + 4$, $129 = 5^3 + 4$.
The next term is $6^3 + 4 = 216 + 4 = 220$.

::: interview
For OA pattern-matching questions framed as "node counts" or "server loads", always check the $n^2$ and $n^3$ baseline progressions. OAs heavily disguise standard sequences behind technical scenarios.
:::

**Answer:** $220$ (c)

## Letter & Alphanumeric Series

**Q10.** Find the next term in the series: BZA, DYC, FXE, ?, JVI
(a) HUG (b) HWG (c) UHG (d) WHG

**Solution:**
Analyze each letter position independently:
First letter: B, D, F, ?, J $\rightarrow$ Steps of $+2$. Next is H.
Second letter: Z, Y, X, ?, V $\rightarrow$ Steps of $-1$. Next is W.
Third letter: A, C, E, ?, I $\rightarrow$ Steps of $+2$. Next is G.
The missing group is HWG.

**Answer:** HWG (b)

**Q11.** What comes next: Q1F, S2E, U6D, W24C, ?
(a) Y120B (b) Z120B (c) Y44B (d) Y88B

**Solution:**
Analyze the three components (Letter, Number, Letter):
First letter: Q (17), S (19), U (21), W (23). Steps of $+2$. Next is Y (25).
Number: $1, 2, 6, 24$. Multipliers are $\times 2, \times 3, \times 4$. Next is $24 \times 5 = 120$.
Third letter: F, E, D, C. Steps of $-1$. Next is B.
The next term is Y120B.

**Answer:** Y120B (a)

**Q12.** Find the missing term: C, G, K, O, ?
(a) R (b) S (c) T (d) U

**Solution:**
Convert letters to their numerical positions:
C $= 3$, G $= 7$, K $= 11$, O $= 15$.
The positions increase by $+4$. The next position is $15 + 4 = 19$.
The 19th letter is S.

**Answer:** S (b)

**Q13.** Find the missing term: SCD, TEF, UGH, ?, WKL
(a) CMN (b) UJI (c) VIJ (d) IJT

**Solution:**
First letter: S, T, U, ?, W. Next is V.
Second and third letters: CD, EF, GH, ?, KL. Next consecutive pair is IJ.
The missing term is VIJ.

**Answer:** VIJ (c)

## Analogy

**Q14.** $11 : 1210 :: 10 : ?$
(a) $910$ (b) $970$ (c) $1000$ (d) $900$

**Solution:**
Analyze the relationship between $11$ and $1210$.
We have $11^3 - 11^2 = 1331 - 121 = 1210$.
Applying the $x^3 - x^2$ rule to $10$:
$10^3 - 10^2 = 1000 - 100 = 900$.

**Answer:** $900$ (d)

**Q15.** $7 : 56 :: 9 : ?$
(a) $63$ (b) $81$ (c) $90$ (d) $99$

**Solution:**
The relationship is $x : x(x+1)$.
$7 \times (7+1) = 7 \times 8 = 56$.
Applying to $9$: $9 \times (9+1) = 9 \times 10 = 90$.

**Answer:** $90$ (c)

**Q16.** BUCKET : ACTVBDJLDFSU :: BONUS : ?
(a) ACMNMOTVRT (b) SUNOB (c) ACNPMOTVRT (d) ACMNMOTURT

**Solution:**
The word BUCKET has 6 letters, and the coded word has 12 letters. Each letter in the original word is replaced by the two letters that immediately precede and succeed it in the alphabet.
B $\rightarrow$ A, C
U $\rightarrow$ T, V
C $\rightarrow$ B, D
K $\rightarrow$ J, L
E $\rightarrow$ D, F
T $\rightarrow$ S, U
Apply this to BONUS:
B $\rightarrow$ A, C
O $\rightarrow$ N, P
N $\rightarrow$ M, O
U $\rightarrow$ T, V
S $\rightarrow$ R, T
The resulting string is ACNPMOTVRT.

**Answer:** ACNPMOTVRT (c)

**Q17.** Odometer : Mileage :: Compass : ?
(a) Speed (b) Hiking (c) Needle (d) Direction

**Solution:**
An odometer is an instrument used to measure mileage (distance).
A compass is an instrument used to determine direction.
The relationship is Instrument : Measurement/Function.

**Answer:** Direction (d)

**Q18.** $68 : 130 :: ? : 350$
(a) $210$ (b) $216$ (c) $222$ (d) $240$

**Solution:**
Analyze the numbers for proximity to perfect cubes.
$68 = 4^3 + 4$.
$130 = 5^3 + 5$.
The right side has $350 = 7^3 + 7$.
The missing term corresponds to $x = 6$, so it should be $6^3 + 6 = 216 + 6 = 222$.

**Answer:** $222$ (c)

**Q19.** Paw : Cat :: Hoof : ?
(a) Lion (b) Horse (c) Elephant (d) Dog

**Solution:**
A cat's foot is called a paw.
A horse's foot is called a hoof.
The relationship is Animal foot : Animal.

**Answer:** Horse (b)

## Classification (Odd One Out)

**Q20.** Find the odd number out: $144, 169, 196, 210, 225, 256$
(a) $144$ (b) $196$ (c) $210$ (d) $225$

**Solution:**
Check for perfect squares.
$144 = 12^2$, $169 = 13^2$, $196 = 14^2$, $225 = 15^2$, $256 = 16^2$.
The number $210$ is not a perfect square.

**Answer:** $210$ (c)

**Q21.** Find the odd group of letters:
(a) BDF (b) JLN (c) PQS (d) VXZ

**Solution:**
Check the letter spacing.
B (+2) D (+2) F.
J (+2) L (+2) N.
P (+1) Q (+2) S.
V (+2) X (+2) Z.
The group PQS does not follow the uniform $+2$ step pattern.

**Answer:** PQS (c)

**Q22.** Choose the word which is least like the other words in the group:
(a) Copper (b) Zinc (c) Brass (d) Aluminum

**Solution:**
Copper, zinc, and aluminum are all pure elements (metals).
Brass is an alloy (a mixture of copper and zinc).
Therefore, brass is the odd one out.

**Answer:** Brass (c)

**Q23.** Find the odd one out: $42, 84, 91, 71, 98$
(a) $42$ (b) $84$ (c) $71$ (d) $91$

**Solution:**
Check divisibility rules. Look at primes.
$42 = 7 \times 6$
$84 = 7 \times 12$
$91 = 7 \times 13$
$98 = 7 \times 14$
The number $71$ is a prime number and not divisible by $7$.

**Answer:** $71$ (c)

**Q24.** Find the odd group out:
(a) $13 - 2197$ (b) $15 - 3375$ (c) $12 - 1728$ (d) $14 - 2746$

**Solution:**
The relationship is $x - x^3$.
$13^3 = 2197$.
$15^3 = 3375$.
$12^3 = 1728$.
$14^3 = 2744$. The given number is $2746$, which is $14^3 + 2$.
Therefore, $14 - 2746$ is the odd group.

**Answer:** $14 - 2746$ (d)

## Magic Squares & Number Grids

**Q25.** A $3\times 3$ magic square is filled with the numbers $1$ to $9$ (each used once) so that every row,
column, and both diagonals have the same sum. What is that common sum, and which number must sit in the centre
cell?
(a) Sum $12$, centre $4$ (b) Sum $15$, centre $5$ (c) Sum $18$, centre $6$ (d) Sum $15$, centre $9$

**Solution:** The total of $1$ through $9$ is $45$, split across $3$ rows of equal sum $S$: $3S = 45 \Rightarrow
S = 15$. The centre cell lies on $4$ lines (a row, a column, and both diagonals). Adding those $4$ lines counts
the centre $4$ times and every other cell once: $4 \times 15 = 60 = 45 + 3 \times(\text{centre})$, so
$\text{centre} = \dfrac{60 - 45}{3} = 5$.

**Answer:** Sum $15$, centre $5$ (b)

::: keypoint
Magic constant of a normal $n\times n$ square (using $1$ to $n^2$) is $\dfrac{n(n^2+1)}{2}$; for $n=3$ that is
$15$. The centre of a $3\times 3$ magic square is always the middle value, $5$.
:::
